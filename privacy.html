Privacy Policy — Jarvis

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains what data Jarvis, a self-hosted Discord music bot, processes when you use it.

1. What data is processed

To function, Jarvis reads the Discord messages and slash commands you send it (such as song requests or playback controls), and your voice channel connection state, in order to play and control audio. This data is used only to carry out your command in real time.

2. What is stored

Jarvis does not maintain a database of user messages, listening history, or personal information. Any data it processes (such as the current queue or playback state) is held in memory only, for as long as it's needed to operate, and is cleared when the bot restarts or the session ends.

3. Third-party sources

Jarvis retrieves audio from third-party platforms (such as YouTube and SoundCloud) to fulfil playback requests. Use of those platforms is subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Data sharing

Jarvis does not sell, share, or transmit any user data to third parties beyond what's required to fetch the requested audio.

5. Hosting

Jarvis runs on infrastructure controlled by the developer. No external analytics or tracking services are used.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of Jarvis after changes means you accept the updated policy.

7. Contact

Questions can be directed to the developer via Discord.
